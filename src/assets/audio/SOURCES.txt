Audio Station Sources
=====================

All audio files in this directory are in the public domain.

Library of Congress National Jukebox Recordings
------------------------------------------------
Pre-1923 recordings that entered the public domain on January 1, 2022
under the Music Modernization Act of 2018.

Source: Library of Congress National Jukebox dataset
        https://data.labs.loc.gov/jukebox/

1. creole-belles-clip.mp3
   Title: "Creole Belles"
   Artist: Vess L. Ossman
   Recorded: 1902
   LOC Item ID: jukebox-213411
   Source file: jukebox-213411_1.mp3
   
2. dill-pickles-clip.mp3
   Title: "Dill Pickles Rag"
   Artist: Arthur Pryor's Band
   Recorded: 1909
   LOC Item ID: jukebox-127191
   Source file: From LOC National Jukebox dataset

3. alexanders-clip.mp3
   Title: "Alexander's Ragtime Band"
   Artist: Victor Military Band
   Recorded: 1911
   LOC Item ID: jukebox-130931
   Source file: From LOC National Jukebox dataset

Each clip is approximately 32 seconds, trimmed from the original recording.

Static/Noise Generation
-----------------------
Radio static and tuning noise are generated in real-time using the Web Audio API
(see index.html). No external audio samples are used for static.
